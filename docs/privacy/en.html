Slivue Privacy Policy

Google Play preparation draft · Package: com.devdooly.slivue
Draft prepared: October 31, 2026 · Effective date: not yet set

This is not a final policy for publication or submission.

The public operator name, privacy contact email and public policy URL have not been decided. This draft describes the planned first Google Play version. It must be reviewed against the final app's permissions, data handling, network activity and backup behavior, and approved with complete operator information. Do not submit it to Google Play or present it as an effective policy in this state.

1. Scope and operator details

This draft applies only to the Google Play distribution of Slivue, com.devdooly.slivue. It does not apply unchanged to the GitHub direct distribution, com.devdooly.notificationedge, whose updater, optional fonts and backup behavior differ.

The two distributions are separate apps and can be installed together. Settings, permissions and notification history from the direct distribution do not automatically transfer to the new Play app.

2. Information and purposes covered by the design

  • Notifications: After you accept the in-app disclosure and grant Android notification access, Slivue reads notification titles, text, displayed senders, app identifiers and names, timestamps, conversation messages and reply actions. These support recent notification display, conversation grouping, app and keyword filters, quick replies and optional edge lighting.
  • Quick replies: Text you choose to send is passed through Android's notification reply action to the original messaging app. That app handles subsequent delivery and retention under its own policy.
  • Preferences: Handle, panel and lighting appearance, selected fonts, detected and excluded apps, blocked keywords and your chosen launch and gesture options are stored in the app's internal storage.
  • Imported fonts: A font file you select through Android's file picker and its filename are copied to internal app storage for display. The Play version is being prepared to use system and imported fonts without a downloadable Google Fonts provider.
  • Consent and pause state: The disclosure version, consent time and processing pause state are recorded on the device. Consent is kept outside Android backup so it cannot authorize notification access on another device.
  • Optional pause-before-opening: If you enable this option and open the panel, Slivue can check active media-session apps and playback states and send a pause command to supported video sessions. This is not microphone or screen recording.

When consent, notification access and the service are active and processing is not paused, Slivue can process notifications while its settings screen is not visible. It does not restore sensitive content hidden by Android or the original app or bypass their protections.

3. Transfers and external services

The planned first Play version processes notification display and filtering on the device without uploading notification content or replies to a Slivue developer server. The Play artifact is being prepared without the GitHub APK self-updater, downloadable fonts, raw notification diagnostic export, advertising, analytics or login SDKs, or its own Internet permission. Until the final artifact, transitive dependencies and actual network activity have been checked, this design is not a verified “no collection or sharing” declaration.

When you send a quick reply or open a notification's original app, Android passes that action to the app you chose. Opening the public policy or a store page in an external browser or store may let that external service process connection information under its own policies. Slivue does not attach notification text to those links.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Notification history is held in app memory with limits of 150 notifications and 50 messages per notification. New notifications may replace older entries.
  • Removing a notification from Android's notification shade and clearing Slivue's history are different operations. The planned controls clear in-memory history when you clear it in Slivue, stop all processing, withdraw consent or revoke notification access.
  • Memory history is lost when the process ends. Android notifications that remain active can be read again if consent and permission conditions are met later. Ending the process is therefore not permanent deletion of the original notifications.
  • Detected apps, blocked keywords and excluded-app preferences may remain until you change or delete them in Slivue or clear its app data in Android settings. Stopping processing or clearing notification history does not necessarily remove appearance and filter preferences.
  • Imported fonts can be removed through the app's font deletion control, Android app-data deletion or app removal. The original file you selected is separate from Slivue's internal copy.

5. Backup and device transfer

The Play version is being prepared with rules excluding internal app settings and files from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Consent records use a separate location that is not backed up. The behavior of manufacturer transfer tools and Android versions still requires testing with the final build; this is not an absolute guarantee that no device-transfer mechanism can ever copy data. The direct distribution has a different existing settings-backup policy.

6. Permissions and your choices

  • Notification access: You choose whether to grant it in Android settings after a separate in-app disclosure and affirmative consent. You may decline and still read settings and this policy, but the notification panel's core features will be limited.
  • Display over other apps: Shows the handle and edge lighting while you use another app. You can revoke it in Android settings.
  • Send notifications: Shows service status and a stop action on supported Android versions. It is separate from permission to read other apps' notifications.
  • Stop and resume processing: The planned settings and service notification let you stop all processing. Resuming requires current consent and actual system permissions.
  • Withdraw consent: You can withdraw consent in the app or revoke Android notification access. New notification processing stops; using it again requires the disclosure and permission conditions to be met.
  • Battery optimization: Reviewing battery restrictions is optional on devices that limit background display. Slivue does not forcibly disable battery protection.

The notification-panel design does not add an accessibility service, SMS or call-log access, microphone, camera, location or broad storage access. You choose an individual font file through the system file picker.

7. Security, children and changes

The design uses internal storage and Android permission boundaries and limits sensitive notification content in logs and support material. No software can promise absolute security. Do not attach real messages, verification codes or unnecessary information about other people to support requests.

No separate Slivue account-creation feature is in scope. The operator must decide the intended age groups and distribution countries and review applicable child-related requirements before release. This draft does not determine a content rating or whether the app targets children.

Material changes to information, purposes, transfers or advertising require an updated policy and store declarations, and a new disclosure or consent when needed. The effective date and change-notice process must be set before final publication.

This pre-release draft was prepared on October 31, 2026. It is not a final submission policy until public operator details and the final Play artifact have been verified.
