Slivue Privacy Policy

Google Play distribution · Package: com.devdooly.slivue
Effective date: November 23, 2026

Notification display and filtering are processed on your device.

This policy distinguishes Slivue's processing from processing by messaging apps, webpages and email services that you choose to use. Notification access is used only after your consent and Android permission.

1. Scope and operator details

This policy applies to the Google Play distribution of Slivue, com.devdooly.slivue. It does not apply unchanged to the GitHub direct distribution, com.devdooly.notificationedge, whose updater, optional fonts and backup behavior differ.

2. Information and purposes

  • Notifications: After you accept the in-app disclosure and grant Android notification access, Slivue reads notification titles, text, displayed senders, app identifiers and names, timestamps, conversation messages and reply actions. These support recent notification display, conversation grouping, app and keyword filters, quick replies and optional edge lighting.
  • Quick replies: Text you enter and choose to send is passed through Android's notification reply action to the original messaging app. That app handles subsequent delivery and retention under its own policy.
  • Preferences: Handle, panel and lighting appearance, selected fonts, detected and excluded apps, blocked keywords and your chosen launch and gesture options are stored in the app's internal storage.
  • Imported fonts: A font file you select through Android's file picker and its filename are copied to internal app storage for display. The Play version uses system and imported fonts without a downloadable Google Fonts provider.
  • Consent and pause state: The disclosure version, consent time and processing pause state are recorded on the device. Consent is stored outside Android backup to avoid reusing a transferred consent record as authorization on another device.
  • Optional pause-before-opening: When this option is enabled and you open the panel, Slivue can check active media-session apps and playback states and send a pause command to supported video sessions. This is not microphone or screen recording.

When current consent, actual notification access and the service are active and processing is not paused, Slivue can process notifications while its settings screen is not visible. It does not restore sensitive content hidden by Android or the original app or bypass their protections.

3. Transfers and external services

The Play version displays and filters notifications on the device and does not provide a feature that automatically uploads notification content or replies to a Slivue developer server. It does not include its own Internet permission, the GitHub APK self-updater, a downloadable-font provider, raw notification diagnostic export, or advertising, analytics or login SDKs. This does not mean that the user-directed transfers to original apps or the external-service processing described below do not occur.

When you send a quick reply or open a notification's original app, Android passes that action to the app you chose. Opening the public policy or a store page in an external browser or store may let that external service process connection information under its own policies. Slivue does not attach notification text to those links.

The public policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit a page, GitHub may process connection information such as your IP address, browser information and access time under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the Slivue app uploading notification content.

Privacy inquiries are received at vmdooly@gmail.com using Google Gmail. The operator processes the sender address and information you choose to email in order to respond, and Google processes this information as the email service provider under the Google Privacy Policy. Do not attach real messages, verification codes or unnecessary information about other people.

Inquiry information retained and managed by the operator is deleted without undue delay once the inquiry-handling purpose has been fulfilled. If retention is legally required, only the information covered by that obligation is stored separately for the required period. This criterion applies to inquiry information retained and managed by the operator; Gmail's own service-side retention and deletion are governed separately by Google's policies and service settings.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Notification history is held in app memory with limits of 150 notifications and 50 messages per notification. New notifications may replace older entries.
  • Removing a notification from Android's notification shade and clearing Slivue's history are different operations. Slivue clears its in-memory history when you clear it in Slivue, stop all processing, withdraw consent or revoke notification access.
  • Memory history is lost when the process ends. Android notifications that remain active can be read again if consent and permission conditions are met later. Ending the process is therefore not permanent deletion of the original app's or Android's notifications.
  • Detected apps, blocked keywords and excluded-app preferences may remain until you change or delete them in Slivue or clear its app data in Android settings. Stopping processing or clearing notification history does not necessarily remove appearance and filter preferences.
  • Imported fonts can be removed through the app's font deletion control, Android app-data deletion or app removal. The original file you selected is separate from Slivue's internal copy.

5. Backup and device transfer

The Play version uses rules excluding internal app settings and files from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and consent records use a separate location that is not backed up. Android versions and manufacturers' transfer tools may behave differently; app settings do not guarantee control over every transfer method or a copy that a user makes separately. The direct distribution has a different existing settings-backup policy.

6. Permissions and your choices

  • Notification access: You choose whether to grant it in Android settings after a separate in-app disclosure and affirmative consent. You may decline and still read settings and this policy, but the notification panel's core features will be limited.
  • Display over other apps: Shows the handle and edge lighting while you use another app. You can revoke it in Android settings.
  • Send notifications: Shows service status and a stop action on supported Android versions. It is separate from permission to read other apps' notifications.
  • Stop and resume processing: You can stop all processing in Slivue settings or the service notification. Resuming requires current consent and actual system permissions.
  • Withdraw consent: You can withdraw consent in the app or revoke Android notification access. New notification processing stops; using it again requires the disclosure and permission conditions to be met.
  • Battery optimization: Reviewing battery restrictions is optional on devices that limit background display. Slivue does not forcibly disable battery protection.

The app does not request an accessibility service, SMS or call-log access, microphone, camera, location or broad storage access for notification display. You choose an individual font file through the system file picker.

7. Security, children and changes

The app uses internal storage and Android permission boundaries and limits sensitive notification content in logs and support material. No software can promise absolute security. Do not attach real messages, verification codes or unnecessary information about other people to support requests.

Slivue does not provide separate account creation or login. The intended users are teenagers and adults in the age groups 13–15, 16–17, and 18 and over. The app is not designed primarily for children under 13. These target-audience age groups are separate from an IARC content rating; being aged 13 or over does not mean exemption from child-related requirements in every distribution country. The operator checks the child- and teen-related requirements in distribution countries and adjusts availability when necessary.

Material changes to information, purposes, transfers or advertising require an updated public policy and store declarations, with necessary in-app notices. A new disclosure or consent is requested when needed, and the changed policy states its effective date.
