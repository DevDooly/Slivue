Slivue direct distribution privacy information

Implementation information for the GitHub APK (com.devdooly.notificationedge), as of October 31, 2026. This is not the separate Google Play build’s policy. A public operator contact and finalized published privacy policy are being prepared separately.

Access and purpose

After explicit in-app consent and Android notification access, Slivue reads app names, senders, titles, message text, timestamps and reply actions to display and filter notifications. Processing can continue when the settings screen is closed, until you stop Slivue. The optional pause-video feature also accesses active media sessions.

Replies and external connections

Quick replies are passed to the original messaging app through its notification reply action. That app handles delivery under its own policies. Slivue has no implementation that uploads notifications or replies to a developer server. Optional Google Fonts may be downloaded by the Google Play services font provider. Update checks and APK downloads you choose use GitHub and allowed HTTPS download hosts. Those providers may process connection information.

Storage and backup

Notification history is held in memory, limited to 150 notifications and 50 messages per notification. Items removed from the original app may remain in Slivue history. Memory is lost when the process ends; active system notifications can be read again. Preferences, detected/excluded apps and blocked keywords are saved in app storage. Imported fonts are copied to internal files. Settings or files may be backed up, restored or transferred according to Android and manufacturer settings. Consent and the processing pause state are stored in an installation-specific no-backup file.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default and expire after 12 hours when enabled. Only some patterns are masked; names or message content may remain. Review the output before copying or sharing it. There is no automatic upload.

Stop, withdraw and delete

Stop Slivue in settings or the service notification pauses processing, removes overlays and clears Slivue’s in-memory history. Withdrawing consent also stops processing. Revoke notification access and display-over-other-apps permission separately in Android settings. Clearing Slivue history does not dismiss notifications in original apps. Remove individual filters and imported fonts in settings. Use Android app storage settings or uninstall to remove saved app data. Manage OS backups separately in your device/account backup settings.

Accounts, advertising and contact

No Slivue account, advertising or analytics SDK, or in-app billing is included. General feature inquiries can be filed in the project’s GitHub Issues. Do not post private notifications, phone numbers, account details or other sensitive information in public issues.
